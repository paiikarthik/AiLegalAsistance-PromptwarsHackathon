RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made and executed at Bangalore on this 1st day of August 2026, by and between:

LESSOR / LANDLORD:
Mr. Rajesh Sharma, S/o Shri Om Prakash Sharma, residing at Flat No. 402, Sunshine Apartments, Indiranagar, Bangalore - 560038, Karnataka (hereinafter referred to as the 'LESSOR', which expression shall include his heirs, legal representatives, and assigns).

AND

LESSEE / TENANT:
Mr. Suresh Kumar, S/o Shri Venkat Raman, residing at No. 15, 2nd Main Road, Koramangala 4th Block, Bangalore - 560034, Karnataka (hereinafter referred to as the 'LESSEE', which expression shall include his heirs and assigns).

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the residential premises situated at Flat No. 204, Green Towers, 100 Feet Road, Indiranagar, Bangalore - 560038, comprising 2 Bedrooms, Hall, Kitchen (hereinafter referred to as the 'SCHEDULE PROPERTY').

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:

1. DURATION:
This agreement shall be valid for a period of 11 (Eleven) months commencing from 1st August 2026 to 30th June 2027.

2. RENT:
The Lessee agrees to pay a monthly rent of Rs. 25,000/- (Rupees Twenty-Five Thousand Only) per month, payable on or before the 5th day of every calendar month via bank transfer to the Lessor's designated bank account.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT:
The Lessee has deposited with the Lessor a sum of Rs. 1,50,000/- (Rupees One Lakh Fifty Thousand Only) as interest-free Security Deposit. The said security deposit shall be refunded by the Lessor to the Lessee at the time of vacating the schedule property, after deducting any arrears of rent, utility bills, or cost of damages to the premises.

4. LOCK-IN PERIOD & PENALTY FOR EARLY TERMINATION:
Both parties agree to a mandatory Lock-in Period of 6 (Six) months. If the Lessee terminates this agreement or vacates the premises prior to the completion of the 6-month Lock-in period, the Lessee shall forfeit the ENTIRE Security Deposit of Rs. 1,50,000/- to the Lessor as liquidated damages.

5. NOTICE PERIOD:
After the completion of the Lock-in period, either party may terminate this agreement by giving 2 (Two) calendar months prior written notice to the other party.

6. UTILITIES AND MAINTENANCE:
The Lessee shall pay all electricity charges, water supply charges, and Monthly Society Maintenance fees of Rs. 3,000/- directly to the Apartment Association. The Lessor shall not be liable for any utility or maintenance costs during the tenancy.

7. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE:
The Lessee shall keep the premises in good and tenantable condition. Minor day-to-day repairs like replacing light bulbs, tap washers, and small plumbing fixtures shall be borne by the Lessee. However, the agreement is silent on major structural seepage or roof waterproofing repairs.

8. RESTRICTIONS:
The Lessee shall use the schedule property exclusively for residential purposes. The Lessee shall not sublet, assign, or transfer the tenancy rights to any third party without prior written consent from the Lessor. No structural alterations or painting changes shall be carried out without written authorization.

9. RENT ESCALATION:
If the agreement is renewed after 11 months, the monthly rent shall be increased by 10% (Ten Percent) over the prevailing rate.

10. JURISDICTION:
This agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Bangalore, Karnataka.
